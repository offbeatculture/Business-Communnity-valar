## Visual Style

- **Dark mode first** — the brand is built around dark, professional aesthetics
- **Warm gradient accents** — red-to-orange gradients for hero sections and key CTAs
- **High contrast** — light text on dark backgrounds for readability
- **Textured feel** — subtle dark textures (carbon fiber, brushed metal look) in key areas
- **Professional & aspirational** — clean, confident, no-nonsense design
- **Rounded corners** — medium border radius (0.625rem / 10px)

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Use Brand Red for primary actions and active states
- Maintain high contrast ratios for readability
- Use dark backgrounds consistently
- Apply warm gradients sparingly for emphasis

**Don't:**
- Use bright/saturated colors for large areas
- Mix too many accent colors in one view
- Use light mode backgrounds (brand is dark-first)
- Use indigo, purple, or cool-toned accents (brand is warm-toned)
